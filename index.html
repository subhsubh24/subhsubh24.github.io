Subh's Home Assistant

A private home-automation setup for one person's apartment, run by Subhodip Mukherjee for his own use. It is not a product or service and is not offered to anyone else.

It connects to the owner's own Google account (read-only Gmail, and the Nest thermostat) so his home can keep track of his receipts, bookings and deliveries and keep the apartment comfortable.
